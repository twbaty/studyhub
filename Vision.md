# StudyHub Vision

## Vision

StudyHub exists to make learning more effective by combining reference material,
guided study, and interactive review into a single portable platform.

The project is built around a simple belief:

> Understanding is more valuable than memorization.

Rather than acting as another flashcard application or exam simulator, StudyHub
is intended to become a reusable learning platform that supports any subject
through structured notes, explanations, and active review.

---

## Long-Term Goals

StudyHub should become a platform capable of supporting multiple disciplines,
including technical certifications, engineering, law, genealogy, martial arts,
and any subject where organized learning is valuable.

Example study tracks include:

- CHFI
- CEDS
- Digital Forensics
- Incident Response
- Accident Reconstruction
- Python
- SQLite
- Networking
- Personal Research Projects

The platform should allow new study tracks to be added without modifying the
application itself.

---

## Success

StudyHub is successful when:

- Learning feels conversational rather than stressful.
- Content remains portable.
- New study guides can be created easily.
- Explanations teach concepts instead of simply revealing answers.
- The software disappears into the background and lets the learner focus on the
  material.

---

## Non-Goals

StudyHub is not intended to become:

- A commercial Learning Management System (LMS)
- A replacement for official training
- A certification shortcut
- A social media platform
- A cloud service requiring accounts or subscriptions

The focus remains personal learning and open educational resources.
